Product Overview

FHG series

The FHG series is engineered to deliver high torque and superior performance compared to standard products. With a torque capacity that is 30% higher, the FHG series is designed to handle heavier loads and provide more powerful output, making it suitable for applications requiring significant force and efficiency.

In addition to its increased torque, the service life of the FHG series is extended by 43%, ensuring long-term reliability and reducing the need for frequent replacements or maintenance.

This extended service life, combined with its high load capacity, makes the FHG series a reliable solution for demanding industries such as heavy machinery, manufacturing, and automotive, where both high performance and durability are essential.
